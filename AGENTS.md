# MagiSprite project instructions

Read ARCHITECTURE.md, DESIGN.md, ART_DIRECTION.md, and ENGINEERING.md before making substantive changes. ARCHITECTURE.md records owner requirements; proposed mechanics in DESIGN.md are not automatically approved.

- Current stage: preproduction. Do not implement gameplay or choose an engine without a subsequent request authorizing that work.
- The owner endorsed only the combat view (`wireframes/03-combat.png`) as the game's visual concept. Its in-scene Sprites are the current character-style reference. The other six views are unapproved historical studies, not production targets.
- Every battle must depict the surrounding area. Whether this uses the same exploration scene instance or an area-matched combat scene remains undecided.
- Combat Sprites need idle body animations, idle emissive effects, and randomized idle emotes. ART_DIRECTION.md contains proposed timing, prioritization, and asset requirements; actual animation assets have not been produced.
- Keep owner decisions distinct from proposals. Approval of the visual example does not approve its numeric balance, team size, exact controls, or every proposed mechanic.
- Preserve the copied ENGINEERING.md unless the owner asks to change it. Its draft status remains as supplied.
- Keep generated concept originals and their prompts available. Use the accepted combat reference for future art; do not infer acceptance of archived studies.
- Do not publish local machine paths, credentials, unrelated personal context, or private conversation identifiers. Preserve source provenance using descriptive titles and content hashes.
- Keep documentation, the gallery's approval labels, and MANIFEST.sha256 synchronized. The manifest covers repository content except .git, ignored files, and itself.
